Fruit Salad – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Fruit Salad is a game made by Vasilis Tsichlakis. This policy explains what the game does with your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information ourselves. The game has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking of its own.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you can choose to watch an ad to get a power-up or a rainbow fruit. Ads are shown only when you ask for one.

The ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, your IP address and approximate location, and how you interact with the ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Information stored on your device

To work, the game saves a few things only on your own device: your top scores, achievements and progress, your settings (music, sound, vibration) and your rainbow fruits and power-ups. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Permissions

Vibration: used to shake your phone during explosions and earthquakes in the game. You can turn it off in the game's settings.

Notifications: if you have played several days in a row, the game can remind you at about 10 pm on a day you haven't played yet, so your streak isn't lost. The reminder is created on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings.

Internet and advertising ID: used only by Google AdMob to load the optional ads. The game itself works without an internet connection; without one, there are simply no ads.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
